Commercial Structure

Clear fees. No hidden complexity.

Our proposal separates commission from operating expenses so owners can see what they earn and what they pay.

01 / Management Fee

Ziman Home charges 15%, 20% or 30% based on the selected package. The percentage applies to accommodation booking revenue actually earned from completed stays, before platform fees and other operating deductions.

Note: Refunds, taxes, refundable deposits and separately charged cleaning fees are excluded from the commission base.

02 / Cleaning Upon Request

Cleaning is arranged upon request only and is not automatically scheduled. The applicable cleaning fee is charged separately through the Airbnb or relevant booking platform. An additional RM30 cleaning charge may be added to the guest booking on the platform when applicable. Cleaning requests remain subject to availability.

03 / Property Setup & Operating Expenses

The owner funds initial furnishing, property preparation, utilities, internet, property repairs, replacements and other approved property expenses. Ziman Home does not charge a separate management onboarding fee.
